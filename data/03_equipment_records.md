# SafeSite demo document: Equipment records

**Fictional training policy. Not a substitute for equipment manuals or qualified supervision.**

Document ID: SS-EQP-003. Issued: 2026-02-10. Policy owner: Maintenance Office.

The fictional Cedar Works site records equipment inspection outcomes in the Digital Asset Register (DAR). Each record contains an asset ID, inspection date, inspector name and status. Defects are escalated to the maintenance supervisor, who decides whether an asset can return to service after authorized review. The equipment-records administrator is Karim Haddad. The monthly administrative review is scheduled on the first Tuesday of each month.

This policy does not describe mechanical repair methods, scaffold assembly instructions, technical load limits or personal protective equipment specifications.
