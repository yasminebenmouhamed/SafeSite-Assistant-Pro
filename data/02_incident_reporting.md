# SafeSite demo document: Incident reporting

**Fictional training policy. Follow your real employer's safety plan and emergency services when needed.**

Document ID: SS-INC-002. Issued: 2026-02-01. Policy owner: HSE Office.

At the fictional Cedar Works site, an incident or near miss must be reported promptly to the on-duty supervisor and to the HSE Office using the internal Incident Notification Form IN-04. The supervisor records the report reference number. The incident log is reviewed every Friday at 15:00 by the HSE coordinator. The fictional incident mailbox is not published in this demo document.

This document is an administrative reporting policy, not medical advice or emergency-response instructions. In an actual emergency, follow the site's emergency procedure and contact qualified responders.
